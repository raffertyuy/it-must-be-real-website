# Rafal — illustration-only production brief

The reader supplies all headings, narration, dialogue and page numbers as editable HTML. Artwork must contain **no text**, no speech bubbles, no labels, no motivational posters, no book mockup, no white text panel, and no UI/navigation controls.

## Output

Create ONE large 3-column × 3-row contact sheet with NINE separate 2:3 PORTRAIT illustrations, reading left-to-right, top-to-bottom. Narrow straight gutters separate the panels for cropping. This is artwork for the left page of a website storybook, NOT an image of a book interface. Target master 2048 × 3072 or higher. Cyber-futuristic cinematic manga/graphic-novel rendering for middle-school readers: detailed environments, expressive but age-appropriate faces, black/navy, cyan rim-light, restrained violet accents. Not chibi or a kiddie cartoon. Match the previously approved dark/cyan and orange character designs.

## Locked cast

- Rafal: youthful Chinese boy, tousled straight black hair, BLACK RECTANGULAR GLASSES in every panel; charcoal hoodie with cyan accents. His background and age are art direction only, not story text.
- Kyan: youthful Nepali boy, curly dark hair, warm brown skin, orange hoodie, NO GLASSES. Keep his hair, face and clothing consistent.
- Dad: clearly adult man, short black hair, glasses, dark casual shirt. He actually plays with Rafal and loses at both Minecraft Bed Wars and Tetris.
- Mom: clearly adult woman, shoulder-length black hair, teal jacket, no lab coat. She takes Rafal to hospital and brings him to the follow-up. The doctor is a separate person in a white coat.

## Non-negotiable hand continuity

The silver splint/imaginary AI device covers ONLY Rafal's anatomical RIGHT LITTLE FINGER: fifth digit, shortest outermost finger, opposite the thumb. Not index, ring finger, thumb, left hand, wrist, whole-hand armour or glove. Use the same slim silver device with two cyan bands throughout. Show an easily countable right hand, PALM FACING THE VIEWER: the THUMB is at viewer RIGHT, and the smallest PINKY is at viewer LEFT. Only that far-left pinky has metal. Keep all five digits anatomically coherent. Prefer this same simple hand orientation whenever the device is prominent. Do not mirror Rafal. Keep the splinted finger straight/protected and off gaming controls. Rafal is not injured before the accident panel; no splint on his hand after the last doctor's clearance.

## Nine portrait panels

0. Cover art only: Rafal and Kyan in a cyan/violet future gaming city; Rafal displays the clearly correct right little-finger device, palm forward. No title.
1. School P.E.: ball just striking the bare right little finger, Rafal in glasses and sports clothes, Kyan concerned. No splint yet. No injury gore.
2. Hospital: Mom stands beside seated Rafal while a separate doctor explains the fitted right-pinky splint. Clearly show Mom supporting him. Abstract medical monitor only; no hand X-ray that could introduce a wrong-finger highlight.
3. At home: Dad dramatically unveils the alleged AI finger; Rafal gives a skeptical look; Kyan investigates. Rafal displays his right palm with the metal solely on the short outermost pinky. No dialogue bubbles.
4. Minecraft Bed Wars: Rafal and Kyan celebrate, Dad at a second computer reacts to losing, floating block islands visible. Rafal's protected right hand rests away from controls with the little-finger device identifiable; do not armour other digits.
5. Geometry Dash: neon cube and spikes on a screen, Rafal focused with glasses, Kyan cheering, Dad comically trying to stay quiet. Splinted right little finger protected, not tapping controls.
6. Tetris: falling tetrominoes on two screens, Rafal pleased after winning, Dad theatrically incredulous, Kyan amused. Consistent right-pinky device; no text on screens.
7. Investigation: Kyan uses a magnifying glass near Rafal's raised right palm while Dad defends his theory. Same exposed thumb at viewer right, silver device only on little finger at viewer left. No notebook writing or word balloons.
8. Ending: Rafal, Kyan, Mom and Dad by a gaming desk, Rafal's bare right hand visible, removed silver splint on desk, Dad looking at his losing game in mock disbelief. No finger device still worn. No text anywhere.

Reject and regenerate any panel with missing Rafal glasses, Kyan glasses, wrong hand/digit, different device or embedded narrative text. Do not claim anatomical correctness from automated file checks; inspect the artwork visually.
